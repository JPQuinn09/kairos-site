Delete your account

Last updated: 11 October 2026

You can delete your Kairos account and everything in it at any time, without reinstalling the app.

In the app

Open Kairos, go to Profile, then choose Delete your account and confirm. This removes your account and every record attached to it, from your phone and from our database: exercise history, urge logs, exposures you wrote, weekly notes, reminder settings, your consent record and your quiz result. Records are deleted, not deactivated, and we cannot recover them afterwards.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email kairosocd@proton.me from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We may ask a question to check it is you. We will do it within one month, and tell you when it is done.

What is kept

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion, apart from backups held by our database provider for a short time before they are overwritten ([[JAMES: Supabase backup retention in days]]). If you joined the waiting list, that is separate: unsubscribe from the link in any waiting-list email, or ask us.
