Kairos

Act now, not when OCD says. A self-guided ERP companion for OCD.

Kairos helps you run Exposure and Response Prevention exercises and see whether the things that set you off are actually losing their grip — not just whether anxiety faded during a single sit.

What it does

  • A short quiz that matches your answers to OCD patterns across 18 subtypes.
  • Graded exposures for each pattern, plus the ability to write your own.
  • A timer that survives backgrounding, so a long exposure can be left and returned to.
  • In-the-moment urge logging, for the work that happens away from a planned session.
  • Per-pattern progress based on your starting distress over time.

What it won't do

It won't reassure you. It won't tell you your fear is unlikely or confirm that you are safe. Reassurance is a compulsion, and feeding it is the opposite of what ERP is for.

Not a medical device. Kairos does not diagnose or treat OCD and is not a substitute for a qualified clinician. If you are in crisis, contact emergency services — 999 in the UK, 911 in the US.

Your data

The app works fully without an account, and without one nothing leaves your device. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. See the Privacy Policy.
