Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Kairos is a self-guided ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention) companion for OCD. What you record in it is about your own mental health, so the short version matters: what you write is stored on your device and in your own account, not readable by any other user, and never sold, shared, or used to advertise to you.

The short version
  • Kairos requires an account. Your exercise history, urge logs and quiz result are stored on your device and backed up to your account so they survive a change of phone.
  • There is no advertising, no ad trackers, and nothing is ever sold. Crash reports and usage data are off unless you turn them on, as two separate choices in Profile.
  • If you sign in, your data syncs privately to your own account so it follows you across your devices.
  • You can export everything, delete your account from inside the app, and deleting the app removes the local copy.

What Kairos stores

Kairos records only what you enter:

  • Quiz results — which OCD patterns your answers matched.
  • Exercise logs — which exposure you did, your distress rating before and after, how long it lasted, any mid-session ratings you chose to log, what you predicted would happen and whether it did, and whether you did anything to make the exposure feel safer.
  • Exposures you write yourself — the title, description, and the response-prevention notes you add.
  • Urge logs — intensity, what happened, an optional pattern or a trigger you name in your own words, and an optional note.
  • Safety screening answers — your answers to the short set of questions asked before the quiz, including whether you are having thoughts of harming yourself. These are kept on your device only. They are never uploaded, never synced to your account, and never included in an export.
  • Weekly notes — if you choose to add them: what a week was like (for example poor sleep, or more stress than usual) and an optional line of your own. These are stored on your device and in your account, like your other records. They are shown back to you beside what you logged that week; Kairos does not analyse them or draw conclusions from them.
  • Settings — light or dark appearance, and your choices about crash reports and usage data. These are kept on your device only.
  • Reminder times — whether the daily practice reminder is on, and the times of any check-ins you have set. These are kept with your account so they come back when you sign in again or change phone. Only the times are stored, nothing about what they are for.

Kairos does not ask for your name, date of birth, address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or health data from other apps. It does not access your camera, microphone, or HealthKit.

Where it is stored

On your device. Everything you enter is written to your phone first, and the app works offline. Deleting the app removes that copy.

In your account. Kairos requires an account, so the records listed above are also stored in our database and sync to your other devices. Each row is bound to your account and protected by row-level security, so no other user can read your data. The database is operated by Supabase, who act as our data processor and host it in the European Union.

On your device only. Your safety screening answers and your settings are never sent to us or stored in your account. If your phone backs itself up to iCloud or Google, that backup may include them. Because they are not in your account, they do not follow you to a new phone, and deleting the app removes them.

Signing out, and sharing a phone

Your records belong to your account, not to the phone. The phone keeps a working copy so the app opens quickly and works without a connection, and that copy is removed when it should no longer be there:

  • When you sign out, the app first sends anything not yet uploaded to your account, then removes your records, safety screening answers, weekly notes and settings from the phone, cancels your reminders and check-ins, and clears the figures shown by any home-screen widgets.
  • If a different account signs in on the same phone, anything left from the previous account is removed before the new account is shown or synced. One person's records are never shown to, or copied into, another person's account.
  • When you sign back in, your records return from your account. So do your reminder times, if notifications are allowed on that phone. Your safety screening answers, your light or dark preference and your choices about crash reports and usage data do not, because they were only ever kept on the phone; you will be asked the screening questions again.

If you sign out with no internet connection, entries made since the last sync cannot be uploaded first and are lost with the local copy. Crash reports and usage data stop when the phone changes hands, and the next person is asked for their own choice.

Accounts and sign-in

Kairos requires an account. It is what lets your history survive a lost or replaced phone, and it is the boundary that keeps your records yours. You can sign in with an email address and password, or with Google. If you use Google sign-in we receive only your email address and basic profile information — Google does not give us access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or anything else, and we do not request it.

We use your email address solely to identify your account and to send account-related messages such as confirmation and password resets. We do not send marketing email to an account address. The waiting list on this website is separate, and only for people who joined it.

The waiting list

Before the app is in the App Store and Google Play, this website has a form where you can leave an email address to be told when it is out. You do not need an account, and it is not linked to one if you later make one.

  • What we keep — the email address, the date you joined, and whether you ticked the box for updates. Nothing else: no name, no IP address, no cookies, and no tracking in the emails.
  • What you get — a confirmation straight away, so you know it worked and can undo it if it wasn't you, then one email when the app is available. If you left the box unticked, your address is deleted once that second email has been sent.
  • Updates — only if you ticked the separate box, which starts unticked. These are occasional emails about how the app is coming along. Your address is kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Leaving — every email, including the confirmation, has a link that deletes your address, or you can email us and we will delete it.

We know that being on a list for an OCD app says something about you. The list is not readable from this website, is never shared or sold, and is used for nothing but the emails described here. Emails come from kairosapp.co.uk and say “Kairos” in the subject, so use an address only you read if that matters to you.

Crash reports and usage data

The app sends two kinds of technical data, and it is worth being exact about what each one can and cannot contain.

Crash reports. When the app crashes, we receive the technical details of the failure — which screen, which version of the app and operating system, and the programming error itself. This is how we find out something is broken for people who would otherwise just delete the app.

Usage events. We record that certain things happened: an exercise was started, an exercise was completed, an urge was logged, the quiz was finished. This tells us where the app is confusing or where people give up, which is the only way to improve it without guessing.

What these can never contain. Usage events record that something happened, never what it was about. They cannot carry which OCD themes you matched, the text of anything you wrote, your distress ratings, the names of exercises you did, or your email address. This is enforced in the code by a fixed list of permitted events and properties — anything outside it is refused and the event is discarded, rather than sent with the sensitive part removed.

Durations are rounded to the nearest 30 seconds rather than recorded exactly, because a precise timing is closer to an identifier than it looks.

Crash reports go to Sentry and usage events to PostHog, both acting as our processors. If you would rather send neither, you can turn both off in the app under Profile, and the app works exactly the same without them.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data. Ever.
  • We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, insurers, or employers.
  • We do not use it to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not use your exercise content, predictions, urge notes, distress ratings, or matched themes for anything other than showing them back to you.
  • We do not run advertising or attribution SDKs. Nothing here feeds an ad network.
  • We do not build a profile of you or attempt to identify you beyond your account.

Notifications

Every notification Kairos shows is created by the app on your own phone. We do not send them from a server, we are not told when one is shown or tapped, and we do not hold a push-notification address for your device. There are three kinds, and the second and third exist only if you switch them on.

  • Exercise in progress — shown while an exposure timer is running, so you can find your way back to it. It contains the title of the exercise.
  • Daily practice reminder — off unless you turn it on in Profile. One notification a day, reading “Time to practise”.
  • Check-ins through the day — off unless you add one in Profile. Up to three a day, at times you choose. Each reads “Checking in — Tap if there is something to note”, and tapping it opens the urge log. The wording is deliberately bare: it does not mention OCD, urges or what the app is for, because a notification can be read by whoever is near your phone.

The notifications themselves are scheduled by your phone. The times you choose are also saved with your account, so they can be put back when you sign in again or move to a new phone. Removing a check-in, turning the reminder off, signing out, or deleting your account cancels them on the phone.

If you have turned usage data on, switching the daily practice reminder on or off is recorded as an event, with no time attached. Check-ins are not recorded at all.

There are no promotional or streak notifications. You can decline or revoke notification permission at any time in your phone's Settings, and the rest of the app works without it.

Home-screen widgets and the lock screen

Kairos offers optional home-screen widgets, and on iPhone a live timer on the lock screen while an exercise is running. If you add them, they show the number of exercises you have completed this week and in total, a small trend line drawn from your starting distress ratings, and — while an exercise is running — its title and timer.

To make this possible the app copies those few figures to a shared area on your phone that the widget can read. They do not leave your device. They are, however, visible to anyone who can see your home screen or lock screen, and an exercise title can say more than you might want it to. The widgets are entirely optional, and removing them removes the display.

Crisis resources

The crisis screen lists helplines and links to outside organisations. Tapping a phone number places a call using your phone, and tapping a link opens that organisation's website. Kairos does not tell those organisations anything about you, and we are not told that you tapped anything.

Your control over your data

  • Export — Profile → Export your data gives you your quiz result, exercise logs, urge logs, the exercises you wrote and your weekly notes, as readable JSON. It does not include your safety screening answers or your settings, which stay on your device.
  • Delete locally — deleting the app removes the local copy from your device, including your safety screening answers, your weekly notes, your settings, and any reminders or check-ins.
  • Delete your account — Profile → Delete your account. This removes your account and every record attached to it, from this device and from our database. Records are deleted, not deactivated, and we cannot recover them afterwards. You can also email us and we will do it for you.
  • Access and correction — you can view and edit your entries in the app at any time.

If you are in the UK or EU, the UK GDPR and GDPR give you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Our lawful basis for processing is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting your account.

Children

Kairos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention

Synced data is kept until you delete your account. Local data is kept until you sign out or delete the app. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts beyond our provider's standard retention window. A waiting-list address is kept until the launch email is sent, or until you unsubscribe if you asked for updates.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects how your data is handled, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app.
