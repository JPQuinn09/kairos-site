What Kairos is

Kairos helps you plan, run, and record ERP exposures, and shows how your distress changes over time. The exercises are generic examples, not a personalised treatment plan. The quiz suggests which patterns your answers resemble; it is not a diagnosis.

Using it safely

  • Work at a level you can actually tolerate. Nothing in the app requires you to do any specific exercise.
  • Stop if an exposure feels unsafe, and speak to a professional.
  • Do not use Kairos in place of urgent help. If you are in danger, contact emergency services — 999 in the UK, 911 in the US — or a helpline on the app's Crisis Support screen.
  • If you are under the care of a clinician, tell them you are using a self-guided tool.
